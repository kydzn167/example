About the project

Discover. Support. Connect.

StudentsBiz is a proposed campus business directory designed to help the school community discover products and support local campus vendors.

🎯

Our purpose

Make student businesses and campus vendor offerings easier to discover through one organized website.

🤝

Our mission

Encourage support for student entrepreneurship and campus vendors by providing a simple online product and business directory.

🌱

Our vision

A connected campus community where small businesses have an opportunity to be seen and supported.

Why StudentsBiz?

Students and teachers may not always know which products are offered by student entrepreneurs or campus vendors. StudentsBiz brings these listings together so visitors can browse business profiles, view product information, and find vendor-provided contact or ordering details.

Who can participate?

  • Students who operate small businesses.
  • Vendors operating in the school canteen.
  • Other campus sellers approved by the project team and school.
  • Students and teachers who want to explore available products.
Important: StudentsBiz is an informational directory in this version. It does not process payments, guarantee product availability, or automatically manage orders.
